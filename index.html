Who is Halle Mary Events?

Founded in 2023 by Mary Hetrick, Halle Mary Events (pronounced like the Academy Award-winning actress Halle Berry) combines over a decade of expertise in event planning and nonprofit fundraising. With a deep understanding of the complexities of event production, Mary has built a reputation for turning challenges into opportunities.

From declining attendance to tight budgets or navigating complex planning committees, Halle Mary Events thrives on solving problems and delivering results. We're more than event planners—we're your dedicated partners, committed to crafting unforgettable experiences with creativity, precision, and expertise.

Mary has called the Valley home for 30 years and holds a BS in Communications from ASU. A devoted Phoenix Suns fan, she enjoys spending time outdoors and has equal love for cats and dogs.
